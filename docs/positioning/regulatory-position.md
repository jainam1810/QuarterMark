# Regulatory position

**The rule: never use a regulatory argument without first checking which jurisdiction the fund
sits in.** Conflating UK and EU rules has already produced two wrong arguments for this
GBP-priced, UK-focused product. See `corrections-register.md` §2 and §3.

## Two separate pitches. Never merge them.

| | UK funds | EU funds |
|---|---|---|
| **Lead with** | **LP / investor operational due diligence** | Regulatory compliance |
| **Why** | Regulation is *reducing* their burden | AIFMD II genuinely tightens obligations |

## UK — regulation is getting lighter, not heavier

`[VERIFIED 2026-08-21]`

- AIFMD II **does not bind UK-authorised AIFMs**.
- **FCA CP26/26 ("FRAME"), published 14 July 2026**, replaces Annex IV reporting and targets a
  **~75% reduction** in reporting burden. Consultation closed 22 September 2026; final policy
  statement 2027; in force 2028.
- FRAME's **£500m NAV threshold** splits "essential" from "enhanced" reporting. **Every fund in
  the QuarterMark target range falls in the lighter tier.** "Small AIFM" is NAV below £750m.
- Current UK regime until then: AIF001 (Manager Report) + AIF002 (Fund Transparency Report)
  via RegData.

**So: do not sell to UK funds on compliance.** Sell on LP diligence — institutional LPs ask how
a manager monitors covenants, and spreadsheet tracking signals operational risk. That argument
holds in every jurisdiction and does not expire.

## EU — the regulatory pitch is real, but the reporting deadline is 2027

`[VERIFIED 2026-08-04 / 2026-08-21]`

- AIFMD II = Directive (EU) 2024/927. Transposition **16 April 2026**; the amended **Article 24
  reporting applies from 16 April 2027**, with ESMA's technical standards due by then. The
  loan-origination reporting fields **do not yet exist in public form**.
- What bites loan-originating AIFs **now**: **Article 15** (20% single-borrower limit where the
  borrower is a financial undertaking/AIF/UCITS, 5% risk retention, 175% open-ended / 300%
  closed-ended leverage caps, originate-to-distribute prohibition) and **Article 23** (investor
  disclosure of originated loan portfolio composition and loan-related fees and charges).
- Current Annex IV: Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 231/2013 Annex IV — two XML files
  against ESMA XSDs, frequency by AUM thresholds (€100m / €500m / €1bn).
- AIFMD II changes do **not** apply to sub-threshold AIFMs.

**So for EU funds the honest line is Article 15 and 23 compliance — not "the reporting deadline
is here."**

## DORA

`[VERIFIED 2026-08-21]` The ESAs published the first list of critical ICT third-party providers
on **18 November 2025** — 19 providers including AWS, Microsoft, Google Cloud, Oracle and SAP,
now under direct ESA oversight. Updated annually.

**DORA is EU-only.** For a UK AIFM the relevant regime is the FCA / Bank of England critical
third parties rules. Useful in an EU fund's DDQ; irrelevant in a UK one.
